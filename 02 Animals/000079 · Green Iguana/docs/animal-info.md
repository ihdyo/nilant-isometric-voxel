## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Vibrant green scaly body with a row of comb-like dorsal spines from neck to tail, a large hanging throat dewlap, a round subtympanic cheek scale, and a long dark-banded tail. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 1.5–2.0 m including the long banded whip-like tail; length 1.5–2 m, weight 4–8 kg [1].

### Lifespan
15–20 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Herbivorous feeder eating tender leaves, flowers, shoots, and ripe tropical fruits. [1].

### Habitat
Tropical rainforest canopies near rivers and streams across Central and South America. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Has a light-sensing 'third eye' (parietal eye) on top of its head that detects shadows of overhead predators. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Can leap from branches 12 metres high into water below and stay submerged for up to 30 minutes. [1].
