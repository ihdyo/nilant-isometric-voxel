## At a glance

- Mammal · Old World monkey family · IUCN Endangered
- Body length: 53–76 cm
- Weight: 7–24 kg
- Lifespan: 15–20 yrs
- Pregnancy: 166 days
- Home turf: Coastal mangrove forests, peat swamps and riverine forests strictly near water on the island of Borneo.
- On the menu: Young mangrove leaves, unripe fruits, seeds and shoots digested in a specialized multi-chambered stomach.
- Fun fact: Adult males grow a fleshy nose over 10 cm long that amplifies honking calls to attract mates and warn rivals.
- Fun fact: They are the finest swimmers among all primates, with partially webbed toes and leaps straight into rivers from 15 meters up.
