## At a glance

- Mammal · Old World monkey family · IUCN Least Concern
- Body length: 47–60 cm
- Weight: 8–14 kg
- Lifespan: 25–30 yrs
- Pregnancy: 173 days
- Home turf: Subtropical evergreen forests, cool temperate broadleaf woods and snowy subalpine mountains across Honshu, Shikoku and Kyushu.
- On the menu: Fruits, seeds, young leaves, buds, tree bark, fungi, insects and crabs.
- Fun fact: Known as 'snow monkeys', they are the northernmost-living non-human primates and famously soak in volcanic hot springs.
- Fun fact: Troops pass down learned cultural behaviors, such as washing sweet potatoes in seawater before eating them.
