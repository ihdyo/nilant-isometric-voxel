## At a glance

- Mammal · Great ape family · IUCN Critically Endangered
- Height: 1.4–1.8 m
- Weight: 70–200 kg
- Lifespan: 35–40 yrs
- Pregnancy: 255 days
- Home turf: Lowland tropical rainforests and swamp forests across Central Africa.
- On the menu: Leaves, stems, shoots, pith, wild fruits, bamboo and termites—consuming up to 18 kg of vegetation daily.
- Fun fact: The largest living primate; adult males develop a saddle of silver-white hair across their backs around age 12.
- Fun fact: A silverback's chest-beat echoes over 1 km through dense jungle and honestly signals his body size to rivals.
