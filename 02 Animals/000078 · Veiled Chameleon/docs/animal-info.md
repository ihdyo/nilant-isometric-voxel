## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Laterally compressed body with vertical yellow and turquoise bands, tall helmet-like casque on the crown, independently rotating turret eyes, zygodactylous grasping feet, and a tightly coiled tail. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 45–60 cm in adult males (including coiled prehensile tail); length 35–60 cm, weight 100–200 g [1].

### Lifespan
5–8 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Insects such as crickets and locusts, supplemented with leaves, blossoms, and fruit for hydration. [1].

### Habitat
Mountainous slopes, valleys, and coastal plateaus of Yemen and southwestern Saudi Arabia. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Its tall head crest (casque) channels morning dew droplets directly down toward the corners of its mouth. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Each turret-like eye moves independently, giving a full 360-degree view before locking onto prey. [1].
