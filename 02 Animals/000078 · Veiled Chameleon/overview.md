## At a glance

- Reptile · Chameleon family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 35–60 cm
- Weight: 100–200 g
- Lifespan: 5–8 yrs
- Clutch: 20–70 eggs
- Home turf: Mountainous slopes, valleys, and coastal plateaus of Yemen and southwestern Saudi Arabia.
- On the menu: Insects such as crickets and locusts, supplemented with leaves, blossoms, and fruit for hydration.
- Fun fact: Its tall head crest (casque) channels morning dew droplets directly down toward the corners of its mouth.
- Fun fact: Each turret-like eye moves independently, giving a full 360-degree view before locking onto prey.
