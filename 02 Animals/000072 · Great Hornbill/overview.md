## At a glance

- Bird · Hornbill family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Length: 95–130 cm
- Wingspan: 1.5–1.8 m
- Weight: 2.1–4 kg
- Lifespan: 35–40 yrs
- Home turf: Tall evergreen rainforests of India, mainland Southeast Asia and Sumatra.
- On the menu: Wild figs, nutmegs, stone fruits, plus tree frogs, lizards, insects and small birds.
- Fun fact: During nesting, the female seals herself inside a tree hollow with mud and fruit pulp—leaving only a narrow slit for the male to pass food!
- Fun fact: It literally 'paints' its white neck and bill bright yellow using golden preen oil from a gland near its tail.
