## At a glance

- Mammal · Camel family · IUCN Critically Endangered
- Height: 1.8–2.3 m
- Weight: 300–1,000 kg
- Lifespan: 35–50 yrs
- Pregnancy: 13 months
- Home turf: Cold rocky deserts, steppes and sand dunes of Central Asia, enduring −40 °C winters and 40 °C summers.
- On the menu: Thorny desert shrubs, dry grasses, twigs and salty plants—plus up to 100 litres of water in one go.
- Fun fact: Its two humps store fat—not water—which converts into energy and hydration during long desert treks.
- Fun fact: Double rows of long eyelashes and closable nostrils seal out fierce Gobi sandstorms.
