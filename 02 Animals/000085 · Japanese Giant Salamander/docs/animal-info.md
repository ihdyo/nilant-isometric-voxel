## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Broad flattened head with tiny lidless eyes, wide mouth, heavy mottled brown body with loose wrinkled lateral skin folds, short stubby legs, and a paddle-like tail. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 1.0–1.5 m (second-largest amphibian in the world); length 1–1.5 m, weight 20–25 kg [1].

### Lifespan
50–80 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Fish, frogs, crabs, crayfish, and aquatic insects ambushed at night. [1].

### Habitat
Cold, fast-flowing mountain streams and clear rivers in southwestern Honshu, Shikoku, and Kyushu in Japan. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Breathes entirely through its wrinkled, folded skin rather than lungs, absorbing oxygen directly from cold stream water. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Known in Japan as osanshouo ('giant pepper fish') because its defensive skin secretion smells like Japanese pepper. [1].
