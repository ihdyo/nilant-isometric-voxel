## At a glance

- Amphibian · Giant salamander family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Length: 1–1.5 m
- Weight: 20–25 kg
- Lifespan: 50–80 yrs
- Clutch: 400–500 eggs
- Home turf: Cold, fast-flowing mountain streams and clear rivers in southwestern Honshu, Shikoku, and Kyushu in Japan.
- On the menu: Fish, frogs, crabs, crayfish, and aquatic insects ambushed at night.
- Fun fact: Breathes entirely through its wrinkled, folded skin rather than lungs, absorbing oxygen directly from cold stream water.
- Fun fact: Known in Japan as osanshouo ('giant pepper fish') because its defensive skin secretion smells like Japanese pepper.
