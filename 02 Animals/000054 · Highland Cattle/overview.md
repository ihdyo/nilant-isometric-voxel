## At a glance

- Mammal · Cattle family · IUCN Domesticated (not evaluated)
- Shoulder: 1.1–1.3 m
- Weight: 420–800 kg
- Lifespan: 15–20 yrs
- Horn span: Up to 1.5 m
- Home turf: Rugged wet moorlands, hills and coastal pastures of the Scottish Highlands and islands.
- On the menu: Coarse moorland grasses, heather, sedges and shrubs that other cattle breeds cannot digest.
- Fun fact: Their iconic shaggy fringe—called a 'dossan' in Scots—shields their eyes from driving rain, snow and midges.
- Fun fact: They grow a double coat (oily outer hair over a downy undercoat) instead of thick back fat to stay warm.
