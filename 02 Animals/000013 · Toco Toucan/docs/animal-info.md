## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Black body with a white throat; a huge orange-yellow bill with a black tip, about a third of the total length but a twentieth of the body mass; blue skin around the eye inside an orange ring; juveniles are duller with a stubbier bill [1].

### Size and weight
About 61 cm long on average, 592–760 g; males are typically larger than females [1].

### Lifespan
Typically 20 years in the wild (up to 26), about 18 in captivity [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Mainly fruit, plus insects and other birds' eggs; it tosses fruit back with the bill and swallows it without using its tongue [1].

### Habitat
Dry, semi-open areas: woodland, savanna and plantations, many in Brazil's cerrado; usually lowland, seen up to 1,750 m in the Andes [1].

### Geographic distribution and native regions
Argentina, Peru, French Guiana, Bolivia, Paraguay, Suriname and Brazil [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Diurnal, arboreal and nomadic, in flocks of about six that follow the fruiting seasons; very loud, with croaks, rattles and bill clacking [1].

### Social structure
Flocks of about six; monogamous pairs, courting with a fruit toss [1].

### Reproduction
Nests in tree cavities; 2–4 eggs incubated by both parents for 15–18 days; chicks fledge at 6–8 weeks [1].

### Predators and threats
Jaguars, snakes, coatis and eagles; the pet trade [1].

## Additional information

### Unique adaptations
The bill is a controllable radiator: blood flow to it rises to dump heat when it is warm and falls to save heat when it is cool [3].

### Ecological role and importance
A seed disperser: the manduvi tree, where hyacinth macaws nest, depends on toucans to spread its seeds [1].

### Interesting facts
- That huge bill is an air conditioner: toucans send warm blood into it to cool down on hot days. [3]
- The biggest bill for its size of any bird: a third of its length, yet only a twentieth of its weight. [1]

### Uncertain or variable information
The 'biggest bill relative to body size of any bird' is ADW's claim [1].
