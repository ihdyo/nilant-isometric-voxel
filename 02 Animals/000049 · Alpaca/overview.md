## At a glance

- Mammal · Camel family · IUCN Domesticated
- Shoulder: 81–99 cm
- Weight: 55–85 kg
- Lifespan: 15–20 yrs
- Pregnancy: 335–345 days
- Home turf: High-altitude Andean wetlands (bofedales) and puna grasslands at 3,500–5,000 m in Peru, Bolivia, Chile and Ecuador.
- On the menu: Tender highland grasses, sedges, hay and leaves clipped gently without uprooting pasture plants.
- Fun fact: Bred for over 5,000 years from the wild vicuña specifically for its luxuriously soft, hypoallergenic fleece—not as a pack animal.
- Fun fact: Unlike the llama's long banana-shaped ears, alpacas have shorter, straight spear-shaped ears and a fluffy wool topknot.
