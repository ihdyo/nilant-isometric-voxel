## At a glance

- Mammal · Cat family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Length: 2.4–3.3 m
- Weight: 126–189 kg
- Pride: 2–40 lions
- Rest: ~20 h/day
- Home turf: Open savannas and grassy plains of sub-Saharan Africa, plus one forest in India.
- On the menu: Zebras, wildebeest, gazelles and buffalo. The lionesses do most of the hunting.
- Fun fact: Every lion has its own pattern of whisker spots, like a fingerprint.
- Fun fact: Lions are the only cats with manes.
