## At a glance

- Reptile · Tortoise family · IUCN Endangered
- Shell: 1.2–1.5 m
- Weight: 150–300 kg
- Lifespan: 100–170 yrs
- Clutch: 2–16 eggs
- Home turf: Volcanic highlands, dry grasslands, and scrublands of the Galapagos Islands in Ecuador.
- On the menu: Grasses, leaves, lichens, berries, and prickly pear cactus pads.
- Fun fact: Can survive up to a full year without food or water by metabolizing stored fat and water reserves.
- Fun fact: Shell shapes match island habitats: dome-shaped on lush highlands and saddle-backed on dry islands to reach high cacti.
