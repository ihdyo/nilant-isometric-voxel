## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Massive domed carapace divided into hexagonal scutes, thick scaly columnar legs, a long wrinkled neck, and a small toothless beak. [1].

### Size and weight
Carapace length 1.2–1.5 m (largest living tortoise species); shell 1.2–1.5 m, weight 150–300 kg [1].

### Lifespan
100–170 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Grasses, leaves, lichens, berries, and prickly pear cactus pads. [1].

### Habitat
Volcanic highlands, dry grasslands, and scrublands of the Galapagos Islands in Ecuador. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Can survive up to a full year without food or water by metabolizing stored fat and water reserves. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Shell shapes match island habitats: dome-shaped on lush highlands and saddle-backed on dry islands to reach high cacti. [1].
