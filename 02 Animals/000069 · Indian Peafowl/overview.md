## At a glance

- Bird · Pheasant family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 1.8–2.3 m
- Weight: 2.7–6 kg
- Lifespan: 15–20 yrs
- Train feathers: Over 200
- Home turf: Open deciduous forests, scrublands, riverbanks and village edges across the Indian subcontinent.
- On the menu: Seeds, berries, grain, insects, small reptiles and young snakes.
- Fun fact: Its famous 1.5-metre 'tail' is actually made of over 200 elongated upper-tail covert feathers—each tipped with a shimmering eyespot!
- Fun fact: When displaying, the male rattles his feathers at 26 Hz, creating low-frequency infrasound vibrations felt by nearby peahens.
