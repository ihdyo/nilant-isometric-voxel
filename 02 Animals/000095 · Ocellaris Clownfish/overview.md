## At a glance

- Ray-finned fish · Damselfish family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 8–11 cm
- Weight: 10–25 g
- Lifespan: 6–10 yrs
- Clutch: 100–1,000 eggs
- Home turf: Warm shallow coral reefs and lagoons across the eastern Indian Ocean, Indonesia, and western Pacific.
- On the menu: Zooplankton, copepods, benthic algae, and food scraps shared by its host sea anemone.
- Fun fact: Coated in a specialized sugar-based mucus layer that prevents its host sea anemone from firing stinging cells.
- Fun fact: Born male; if the dominant breeding female dies, the largest male permanently transforms into the new female.
