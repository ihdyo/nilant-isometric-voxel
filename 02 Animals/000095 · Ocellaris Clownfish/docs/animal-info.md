## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Bright tangerine-orange oval fish crossed by three crisp white vertical bars edged in thin black lines, with rounded black-trimmed fins. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 8–11 cm; length 8–11 cm, weight 10–25 g [1].

### Lifespan
6–10 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Zooplankton, copepods, benthic algae, and food scraps shared by its host sea anemone. [1].

### Habitat
Warm shallow coral reefs and lagoons across the eastern Indian Ocean, Indonesia, and western Pacific. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Coated in a specialized sugar-based mucus layer that prevents its host sea anemone from firing stinging cells. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Born male; if the dominant breeding female dies, the largest male permanently transforms into the new female. [1].
