## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Massive armored body with rows of bony dorsal scutes, a broad heavy snout with interlocking conical teeth, high-set golden eyes, webbed hind feet, and a powerful laterally flattened tail. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 4.3–5.2 m in adult males, up to 6+ m (largest living reptile); length 3–6 m, weight 200–1,000 kg [1].

### Lifespan
70+ yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Fish, crabs, turtles, birds, wild boars, and large mammals ambushed at the water's edge. [1].

### Habitat
Brackish estuaries, mangrove swamps, coastal rivers, and open seas from eastern India through Southeast Asia to northern Australia. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Delivers the strongest measured bite force of any living animal, exceeding 16,000 newtons. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Special salt glands on its tongue excrete excess salt, allowing it to travel hundreds of kilometres across open ocean. [1].
