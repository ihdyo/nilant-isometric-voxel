## At a glance

- Mammal · Squirrel family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 34–43 cm
- Weight: 250–340 g
- Lifespan: 3–7 yrs
- Leap: Up to 2 m
- Home turf: Coniferous and broadleaf woodlands across Europe and northern Asia.
- On the menu: Pine cones, hazelnuts, acorns, tree buds, berries and fungi cached for winter.
- Fun fact: Its bushy tail acts as a rudder while leaping between branches and a warm blanket wrapped around its body while sleeping.
- Fun fact: In winter it grows dramatic long tufts of fur on the tips of its ears that shed each spring.
