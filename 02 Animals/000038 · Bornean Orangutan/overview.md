## At a glance

- Mammal · Great ape family · IUCN Critically Endangered
- Height: 1.1–1.4 m
- Weight: 30–100 kg
- Lifespan: 35–45 yrs
- Pregnancy: 245 days
- Home turf: Lowland tropical dipterocarp rainforests and peat-swamp forests across the island of Borneo.
- On the menu: Durians, figs, jackfruit, lychees, young leaves, bark, honey and termites—eating over 400 plant species.
- Fun fact: The largest tree-dwelling mammal on Earth, weaving a fresh leafy sleeping nest high in the canopy every night.
- Fun fact: Its name comes from Malay 'orang hutan', meaning 'person of the forest'; arm span reaches over 2 meters.
