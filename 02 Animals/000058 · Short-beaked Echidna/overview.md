## At a glance

- Mammal · Monotreme · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 35–53 cm
- Weight: 2–7 kg
- Lifespan: Up to 45 yrs
- Tongue: 18 cm long
- Home turf: Forests, deserts, grasslands and mountain slopes across Australia and lowland New Guinea.
- On the menu: Ants, termites and earthworms lapped up with a sticky 18-centimetre tongue.
- Fun fact: Like the platypus, it lays a single soft-shelled egg—which it incubates inside a temporary belly pouch!
- Fun fact: When startled, it curls into a spiky ball or digs straight downward into the soil in seconds.
