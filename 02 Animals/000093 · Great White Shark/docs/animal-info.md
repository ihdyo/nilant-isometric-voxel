## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Torpedo-shaped countershaded body—slate-grey above and white below—with a pointed conical snout, rows of serrated triangular teeth, five gill slits, tall dorsal fin, and crescent tail fin. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 4–6 m; length 4–6 m, weight 680–1,100 kg [1].

### Lifespan
70+ yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Fish and rays as juveniles; seals, sea lions, dolphins, and whale carrion as large adults. [1].

### Habitat
Temperate and subtropical coastal and offshore waters worldwide, especially off California, South Africa, and southern Australia. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Electroreceptors in its snout (ampullae of Lorenzini) detect tiny electrical pulses from a prey's heartbeat. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Keeps its swimming muscles warmer than surrounding cold ocean water, enabling explosive breaches above the surface. [1].
