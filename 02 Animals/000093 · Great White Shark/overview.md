## At a glance

- Cartilaginous fish · Mackerel shark family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Length: 4–6 m
- Weight: 680–1,100 kg
- Lifespan: 70+ yrs
- Gestation: 11–18 months
- Home turf: Temperate and subtropical coastal and offshore waters worldwide, especially off California, South Africa, and southern Australia.
- On the menu: Fish and rays as juveniles; seals, sea lions, dolphins, and whale carrion as large adults.
- Fun fact: Electroreceptors in its snout (ampullae of Lorenzini) detect tiny electrical pulses from a prey's heartbeat.
- Fun fact: Keeps its swimming muscles warmer than surrounding cold ocean water, enabling explosive breaches above the surface.
