## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Stocky black-and-white whale with bumpy tubercles on the snout, extraordinarily long scalloped white pectoral flippers, a humped dorsal fin, and serrated tail flukes. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 12–16 m with pectoral flippers up to 5 m long; length 12–16 m, weight 25–30 t [1].

### Lifespan
45–50 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Krill and small schooling fish such as herring, capelin, and sand lance. [1].

### Habitat
All major oceans, migrating up to 8,000 km between polar feeding waters and tropical breeding lagoons. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Hunts in coordinated groups by blowing spiraling 'bubble nets' that herd fish into tight traps. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Males compose complex, evolving songs lasting up to 20 minutes that spread across entire ocean basins. [1].
