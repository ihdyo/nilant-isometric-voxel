## At a glance

- Mammal · Baleen whale family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 12–16 m
- Weight: 25–30 t
- Lifespan: 45–50 yrs
- Pregnancy: 11.5 months
- Home turf: All major oceans, migrating up to 8,000 km between polar feeding waters and tropical breeding lagoons.
- On the menu: Krill and small schooling fish such as herring, capelin, and sand lance.
- Fun fact: Hunts in coordinated groups by blowing spiraling 'bubble nets' that herd fish into tight traps.
- Fun fact: Males compose complex, evolving songs lasting up to 20 minutes that spread across entire ocean basins.
