## At a glance

- Bird · Shoebill family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Height: 1.1–1.4 m
- Wingspan: 2.3–2.6 m
- Weight: 4–7 kg
- Bill length: Up to 24 cm
- Home turf: Vast freshwater papyrus swamps and marshes of East-Central Africa.
- On the menu: Lungfish, bichirs, tilapia, water snakes and baby crocodiles ambushed in shallow swamp water.
- Fun fact: It stands completely motionless like a prehistoric statue for hours, then lunges forward to snap up lungfish with its 24-cm clog bill.
- Fun fact: Instead of singing, it greets its mate with rapid-fire bill-clattering that echoes across the swamp like a machine gun.
