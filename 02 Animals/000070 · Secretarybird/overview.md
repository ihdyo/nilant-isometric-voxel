## At a glance

- Bird · Secretarybird family · IUCN Endangered
- Height: 1.2–1.5 m
- Wingspan: 1.9–2.1 m
- Weight: 3.3–4.3 kg
- Kick force: 5× body weight
- Home turf: Open grasslands, savannas and shrublands across sub-Saharan Africa.
- On the menu: Snakes (including cobras and puff adders), locusts, lizards, rodents and frogs stomped with lethal kicks.
- Fun fact: Instead of diving from the air, it hunts on foot—delivering kicks with 5 times its body weight in just 15 milliseconds!
- Fun fact: Its name likely comes from its crest of long black quill feathers, resembling 19th-century clerks with quill pens tucked behind their ears.
