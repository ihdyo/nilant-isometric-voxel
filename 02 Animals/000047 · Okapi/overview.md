## At a glance

- Mammal · Giraffe family · IUCN Endangered
- Body length: 1.9–2.5 m
- Weight: 200–350 kg
- Lifespan: 20–30 yrs
- Pregnancy: 14–15 months
- Home turf: Dense lowland tropical rainforests—especially the Ituri Forest—in the northeastern Democratic Republic of the Congo.
- On the menu: Tree leaves, buds, shoots, ferns, fruits and fungi stripped with its long dark-blue prehensile tongue.
- Fun fact: Despite its zebra-striped legs, the okapi is the giraffe's only living relative and shares its long blue tongue and skin-covered ossicones.
- Fun fact: Its tongue is up to 45 cm long—long enough to wash its own eyelids and clean inside its ears.
