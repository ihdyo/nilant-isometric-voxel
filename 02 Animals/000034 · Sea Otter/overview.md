## At a glance

- Mammal · Weasel family · IUCN Endangered
- Total length: 1–1.5 m
- Weight: 14–45 kg
- Lifespan: 15–20 yrs
- Pregnancy: 6–8 months
- Home turf: Shallow coastal kelp forests and rocky reefs along the northern Pacific Ocean.
- On the menu: Sea urchins, abalones, clams, mussels, crabs and sea stars cracked open on its chest using a flat stone.
- Fun fact: It has the densest fur of any animal—up to 150,000 hairs per square centimeter—trapping air instead of blubber for warmth.
- Fun fact: Sea otters wrap themselves in kelp fronds while sleeping at sea so they do not drift away.
