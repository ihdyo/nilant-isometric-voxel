## At a glance

- Mammal · Lemur family · IUCN Endangered
- Body length: 39–46 cm
- Tail length: 56–63 cm
- Weight: 2.2–3.5 kg
- Lifespan: 16–19 yrs
- Home turf: Gallery forests, spiny bush, dry scrub and rocky canyons in southern and southwestern Madagascar.
- On the menu: Tamarind pods, figs, leaves, flowers, bark, sap and occasional insects.
- Fun fact: Its iconic black-and-white tail is longer than its body and held high like a flag to keep the troop together.
- Fun fact: In the morning sun, ring-tailed lemurs sit upright with arms spread wide like tiny sun-worshippers to warm up.
