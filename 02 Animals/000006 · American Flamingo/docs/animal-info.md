## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
A tall wading bird with long pink legs, a long S-shaped neck, coral-pink plumage, black flight feathers and a sharply bent bill with a black tip [1].

### Size and weight
About 1.2 m tall; wingspan 140–165 cm; weight 2.1–4.1 kg [1].

### Lifespan
About 25 years on average in the wild (maximum 44); about 30 in captivity [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Filter-feeds with the head upside down, pumping water through the bill; eats organic ooze, worms, molluscs, crustaceans, insects, small fish and some plants, and stirs sediment with its feet [1].

### Habitat
Hypersaline estuaries, coastal mudflats and lagoons [1].

### Geographic distribution and native regions
The Caribbean, northern South America, the Yucatán Peninsula and Galápagos [1][2].

### Habits and behaviour
Highly colonial; flocks of thousands [1]. Breeding success rose when researchers placed mirrors to make flocks look larger [1].

### Social structure
Colonies of thousands to tens of thousands [1].

### Reproduction
One large egg a year on a mud nest about 30 cm high; both parents incubate for 28–32 days and feed the chick red 'crop milk' [1].

### Predators and threats
Eggs and young are taken by gulls, vultures and foxes; humans hunt adults and eggs in places [1].

## Additional information

### Unique adaptations
The reversed bill works when the head is upside down; carotenoid pigments from the diet colour the plumage and legs [1].

### Ecological role and importance
A filter feeder of shallow saline waters [1].

### Interesting facts
- Its pink comes from its food! Pigments in tiny crustaceans tint the feathers and even the legs. [1]
- It feeds with its head upside down, so its bent bill works the right way up. [1]

### Uncertain or variable information
The ADW page for P. ruber also covers the greater flamingo (P. roseus, formerly lumped with it); its size and lifespan figures may mix the two species [1]. Status: Least Concern, population increasing (BirdLife International 2020) [2].
