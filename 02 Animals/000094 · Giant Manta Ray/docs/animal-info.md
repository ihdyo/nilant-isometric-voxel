## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Broad diamond-shaped disc with wide triangular wing-like pectoral fins, jet-black back with white T-shaped shoulder markings, two forward-projecting cephalic horn fins, and a slender whip tail. [1].

### Size and weight
Disc width (wingspan) 4.5–7 m (largest ray species in the world); wingspan 4.5–7 m, weight 1.3–2 t [1].

### Lifespan
40+ yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Zooplankton, krill, and fish larvae funneled into its wide mouth by rolled cephalic fins. [1].

### Habitat
Tropical, subtropical, and temperate pelagic oceans, coral reefs, and productive upwellings worldwide. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Possesses the largest brain-to-body ratio of any fish and passes mirror self-recognition behavioral tests. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Each manta ray has a unique constellation of dark spots on its white belly used by researchers to identify individuals. [1].
