## At a glance

- Mammal · Anteater family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Length: 1.8–2.4 m
- Weight: 18–41 kg
- Lifespan: 14–16 yrs
- Tongue: 60 cm long
- Home turf: Grasslands, savannas, wetlands and tropical forests of Central and South America.
- On the menu: Up to 30,000 ants and termites a day, flicked up at 150 times per minute with a sticky 60-cm tongue.
- Fun fact: Completely toothless—it crushes insects against hard ridges on the roof of its mouth and muscular stomach.
- Fun fact: It walks on its knuckles with its huge front claws tucked inward so they stay razor-sharp for tearing termite mounds.
