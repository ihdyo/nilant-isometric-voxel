## At a glance

- Mammal · Monodontid whale family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 4–5 m
- Weight: 0.8–1.6 t
- Lifespan: 50 yrs
- Pregnancy: 14–15 months
- Home turf: Cold Arctic waters, fjords, and pack-ice leads around Canada, Greenland, Norway, and Russia.
- On the menu: Greenland halibut, Arctic cod, squid, and shrimp caught during deep winter dives.
- Fun fact: Its famous spiral 'horn' is actually an elongated upper-left canine tooth packed with up to 10 million nerve endings.
- Fun fact: Regularly dives deeper than 1,500 metres beneath dense Arctic sea ice to feed on deep-water halibut.
