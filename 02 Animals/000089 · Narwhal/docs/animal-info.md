## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Stocky mottled grey-and-white body without a dorsal fin, blunt rounded forehead, small upturned flippers, fan-shaped tail flukes, and a long straight counter-clockwise spiral tusk. [1].

### Size and weight
Body length 4–5 m, plus spiral ivory tusk up to 2–3 m; length 4–5 m, weight 0.8–1.6 t [1].

### Lifespan
50 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Greenland halibut, Arctic cod, squid, and shrimp caught during deep winter dives. [1].

### Habitat
Cold Arctic waters, fjords, and pack-ice leads around Canada, Greenland, Norway, and Russia. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Its famous spiral 'horn' is actually an elongated upper-left canine tooth packed with up to 10 million nerve endings. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Regularly dives deeper than 1,500 metres beneath dense Arctic sea ice to feed on deep-water halibut. [1].
