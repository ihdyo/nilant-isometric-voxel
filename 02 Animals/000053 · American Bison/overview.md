## At a glance

- Mammal · Cattle family · IUCN Near Threatened
- Shoulder: 1.5–2 m
- Weight: 318–1,000 kg
- Lifespan: 15–20 yrs
- Top speed: 56 km/h
- Home turf: Open grasslands, prairies, river valleys and plains across North America.
- On the menu: Prairie grasses and sedges, grazed across vast open plains throughout the year.
- Fun fact: North America's heaviest land mammal—despite weighing nearly a tonne, it can sprint at 56 km/h and jump 1.8 metres high.
- Fun fact: Their massive head and shoulder hump act like a snowplow to sweep away deep winter snow from grass.
