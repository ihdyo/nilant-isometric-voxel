## At a glance

- Bird · Barn owl family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 33–39 cm
- Wingspan: 80–95 cm
- Weight: 290–460 g
- Lifespan: 4–10 yrs
- Home turf: Open farmlands, grasslands, forest edges, barns and palm plantations worldwide.
- On the menu: Mice, voles, rats and shrews hunted in total darkness by sound alone.
- Fun fact: Its heart-shaped facial disc acts like a satellite dish, funneling faint rustling sounds to asymmetrically placed ears.
- Fun fact: It can pinpoint and catch a mouse hidden completely under snow or grass in pitch darkness.
