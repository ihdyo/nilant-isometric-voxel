## At a glance

- Bird · Kiwi family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Length: 35–45 cm
- Weight: 1.4–4 kg
- Lifespan: 25–50 yrs
- Egg size: ~20% of body
- Home turf: Subtropical and temperate native forests, scrublands and pine plantations of New Zealand's North Island.
- On the menu: Earthworms, beetle larvae, cicadas, spiders and fallen berries sniffed out underground.
- Fun fact: The only bird in the world with nostrils at the very tip of its long beak—allowing it to smell earthworms deep underground!
- Fun fact: Females lay one of the largest eggs relative to body size of any bird, weighing up to 20% of the mother's body weight.
