## At a glance

- Bird · Cassowary family · IUCN Least Concern
- Height: 1.5–1.8 m
- Weight: 29–58 kg
- Lifespan: 40–50 yrs
- Top speed: 50 km/h
- Home turf: Dense tropical rainforests and swamp forests of New Guinea, nearby islands and northeastern Australia.
- On the menu: Hundreds of species of fallen rainforest fruits swallowed whole, plus fungi, insects and snails.
- Fun fact: The world's third-tallest and second-heaviest living bird—each three-toed foot sports a 12-centimetre dagger-like inner claw!
- Fun fact: As a 'rainforest gardener', over 200 tropical tree species depend on cassowaries to swallow and disperse their large seeds.
