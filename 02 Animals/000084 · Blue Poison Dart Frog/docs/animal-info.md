## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Hunched compact body in brilliant azure-blue skin dotted with irregular black spots, darker cobalt limbs with disc-tipped toes, and large dark glossy eyes. [1].

### Size and weight
Snout-to-vent length 3–4.5 cm; length 3–4.5 cm, weight 3–8 g [1].

### Lifespan
4–6 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Ants, mites, termites, beetles, and tiny flies on the moist forest floor. [1].

### Habitat
Isolated forest islands surrounded by the Sipaliwini savanna in southern Suriname. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Its electric-blue skin and dark spots act as warning coloration, and each frog's spot pattern is unique like a fingerprint. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Acquires its skin toxins from eating wild ants and mites; captive frogs fed fruit flies become completely harmless. [1].
