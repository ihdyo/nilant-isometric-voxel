## At a glance

- Amphibian · Poison dart frog family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 3–4.5 cm
- Weight: 3–8 g
- Lifespan: 4–6 yrs
- Clutch: 5–10 eggs
- Home turf: Isolated forest islands surrounded by the Sipaliwini savanna in southern Suriname.
- On the menu: Ants, mites, termites, beetles, and tiny flies on the moist forest floor.
- Fun fact: Its electric-blue skin and dark spots act as warning coloration, and each frog's spot pattern is unique like a fingerprint.
- Fun fact: Acquires its skin toxins from eating wild ants and mites; captive frogs fed fruit flies become completely harmless.
