## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Tawny-orange wings crisscrossed by bold black veins and framed by a double row of white marginal spots, with a white-spotted black thorax and clubbed antennae. [1].

### Size and weight
Wingspan 8.9–10.2 cm; wingspan 9–10 cm, weight 0.5 g [1].

### Lifespan
2–9 months [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Milkweed leaves as caterpillars; flower nectar from asters, goldenrod, and milkweed as adults. [1].

### Habitat
Open meadows, prairies, and overwintering oyamel fir forests across North America. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Completes an epic multi-generational migration of up to 4,800 km between Canada and central Mexico. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Caterpillars store bitter cardiac glycosides from milkweed plants, making adult butterflies toxic to birds. [1].
