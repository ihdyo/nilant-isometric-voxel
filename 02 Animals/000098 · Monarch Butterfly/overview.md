## At a glance

- Insect · Brush-footed butterfly family · IUCN Least Concern
- Wingspan: 9–10 cm
- Weight: 0.5 g
- Lifespan: 2–9 months
- Eggs: 300–500
- Home turf: Open meadows, prairies, and overwintering oyamel fir forests across North America.
- On the menu: Milkweed leaves as caterpillars; flower nectar from asters, goldenrod, and milkweed as adults.
- Fun fact: Completes an epic multi-generational migration of up to 4,800 km between Canada and central Mexico.
- Fun fact: Caterpillars store bitter cardiac glycosides from milkweed plants, making adult butterflies toxic to birds.
