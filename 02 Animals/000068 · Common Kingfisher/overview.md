## At a glance

- Bird · Kingfisher family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 16–17 cm
- Wingspan: 24–26 cm
- Weight: 34–46 g
- Lifespan: 6–15 yrs
- Home turf: Clear, slow-flowing rivers, streams, canals and reed-fringed lakes across Europe, Asia and North Africa.
- On the menu: Minnows, sticklebacks and aquatic insects caught in lightning-fast headfirst plunges.
- Fun fact: Its dazzling turquoise feathers contain no blue pigment—the colour comes from microscopic nanostructures scattering light!
- Fun fact: Its streamlined beak inspired the nose cone of Japan's Shinkansen bullet train to eliminate tunnel sonic booms.
