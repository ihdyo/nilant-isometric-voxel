## At a glance

- Mammal · Monotreme · IUCN Near Threatened
- Length: 43–60 cm
- Weight: 0.7–2.4 kg
- Lifespan: 12–17 yrs
- Dive time: Up to 2 mins
- Home turf: Freshwater rivers, cool mountain streams and lakes across eastern Australia and Tasmania.
- On the menu: Freshwater shrimp, insect larvae, worms and crayfish detected underwater with its electroreceptive bill.
- Fun fact: One of only five egg-laying mammals (monotremes) on Earth—and males carry a venomous spur on each hind ankle!
- Fun fact: It hunts with eyes, ears and nostrils shut tight, sensing the tiny electrical pulses of prey muscles with its bill.
