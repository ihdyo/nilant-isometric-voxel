## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Massive wrinkled cinnamon-brown pinniped body, broad square muzzle bristling with stiff cream whiskers, two long downward-pointing ivory tusks, and four wide flippers. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 2.7–3.6 m in adult males; length 2.7–3.6 m, weight 800–1,700 kg [1].

### Lifespan
40 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Clams, mussels, cockles, sea cucumbers, and benthic invertebrates suctioned from the seafloor. [1].

### Habitat
Shallow Arctic continental shelves, sea ice floes, and rocky coastal haul-outs across the Arctic Ocean. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Uses hundreds of sensitive snout whiskers (vibrissae) to feel buried clams in dark, muddy Arctic seabeds. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Uses its long ivory tusks (up to 1 m) to haul its massive body out of freezing water onto ice floes. [1].
