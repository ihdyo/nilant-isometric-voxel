## At a glance

- Mammal · Walrus family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Length: 2.7–3.6 m
- Weight: 800–1,700 kg
- Lifespan: 40 yrs
- Pregnancy: 15–16 months
- Home turf: Shallow Arctic continental shelves, sea ice floes, and rocky coastal haul-outs across the Arctic Ocean.
- On the menu: Clams, mussels, cockles, sea cucumbers, and benthic invertebrates suctioned from the seafloor.
- Fun fact: Uses hundreds of sensitive snout whiskers (vibrissae) to feel buried clams in dark, muddy Arctic seabeds.
- Fun fact: Uses its long ivory tusks (up to 1 m) to haul its massive body out of freezing water onto ice floes.
