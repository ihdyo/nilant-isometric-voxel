## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Plump barrel-shaped grey-brown body, whiskered cleft upper lip, small paddle-like forelimbs with tiny nails, and a broad flat rounded paddle tail. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 2.7–3.5 m; length 2.7–3.5 m, weight 400–600 kg [1].

### Lifespan
40–60 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Seagrasses, water hyacinth, mangrove leaves, and freshwater aquatic plants—eating up to 10% of its body weight daily. [1].

### Habitat
Warm, shallow coastal waters, estuaries, mangrove creeks, and freshwater springs from Florida to Brazil. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Replaces its molars continuously throughout life ('marching molars') as abrasive plants wear them down. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Closest living terrestrial relatives are elephants and hyraxes, sharing prehensile upper lips and toenail-like flipper nails. [1].
