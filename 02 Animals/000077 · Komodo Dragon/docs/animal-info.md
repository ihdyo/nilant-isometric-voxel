## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Heavy muscular body covered in armoured osteoderm scales, bowed powerful limbs with curved claws, thick muscular tail, and a long forked yellow tongue. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 2–3 m from snout to tail tip (heaviest living lizard); length 2–3 m, weight 70–90 kg [1].

### Lifespan
30–50 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Deer, wild pigs, water buffalo, carrion, and smaller reptiles, hunted with serrated teeth and venom glands. [1].

### Habitat
Dry tropical savannas, monsoon forests, and coastal scrublands on Komodo, Rinca, Flores, and Gili Motang in Indonesia. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
The heaviest living lizard on Earth, capable of eating up to 80 percent of its body weight in a single meal. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Uses a deeply forked yellow tongue to sample airborne scent molecules from up to 4 km away. [1].
