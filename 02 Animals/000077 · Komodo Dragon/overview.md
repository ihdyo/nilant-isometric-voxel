## At a glance

- Reptile · Monitor lizard family · IUCN Endangered
- Length: 2–3 m
- Weight: 70–90 kg
- Lifespan: 30–50 yrs
- Clutch: 15–30 eggs
- Home turf: Dry tropical savannas, monsoon forests, and coastal scrublands on Komodo, Rinca, Flores, and Gili Motang in Indonesia.
- On the menu: Deer, wild pigs, water buffalo, carrion, and smaller reptiles, hunted with serrated teeth and venom glands.
- Fun fact: The heaviest living lizard on Earth, capable of eating up to 80 percent of its body weight in a single meal.
- Fun fact: Uses a deeply forked yellow tongue to sample airborne scent molecules from up to 4 km away.
