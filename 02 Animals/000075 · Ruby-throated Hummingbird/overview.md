## At a glance

- Bird · Hummingbird family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 7–9 cm
- Wingspan: 8–11 cm
- Weight: 2–6 g
- Wingbeats: 53 per sec
- Home turf: Woodland edges, meadows, orchards and flower gardens across eastern North America, wintering in Central America.
- On the menu: Flower nectar (eating up to twice its body weight daily) plus tiny spiders, gnats and aphids.
- Fun fact: It beats its wings 53 times per second and is the only bird family able to hover motionless and fly backwards or upside down!
- Fun fact: Despite weighing less than a nickel (3 grams), many migrate non-stop 800 km across the open Gulf of Mexico.
