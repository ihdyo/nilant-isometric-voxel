## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Arboreal and terrestrial pangolin covered from forehead to tail tip in overlapping olive-brown keratin scales, with a prehensile tail, narrow conical head, and pale hairless belly. [1]

### Size and weight
Total length 0.9–1.2 m (tail 40–55 cm); weight 4–7 kg. [1]

### Lifespan
15–20 years (poorly known in the wild due to elusive nocturnal habits). [1]

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Specializes on arboreal and terrestrial ants and termites, using strong sense of smell and a tongue anchored deep in the chest cavity. [1]

### Habitat
Lowland tropical rainforests, dipterocarp forests, mangroves, and plantations across Sundaland. [1]

### Geographic distribution and native regions
Southeast Asia: Indonesia (Sumatra, Java, Kalimantan), Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia, and Myanmar. [1]

### Habits and behaviour
Nocturnal and semi-arboreal; climbs trees using its prehensile tail and sleeps in tree hollows or burrows. [1]

### Social structure
Solitary; mother carries single baby riding on the base of her tail. [1]

### Reproduction
Gestation ~130 days; single scale-softened pup born in a burrow or tree hollow. [1]

### Predators and threats
Critically Endangered due to intense illegal poaching for international scale and meat trafficking, plus deforestation. [1, 2]

## Additional information

### Unique adaptations
Thick eyelids and closable nostrils/ears protect against biting ants; pungent anal glands deter predators. [1]

### Ecological role and importance
Vital natural regulator of forest ant and termite populations across Southeast Asia. [1]

### Interesting facts
- The world's only truly scaly mammal—its overlapping armour scales are made of keratin, the same protein as human fingernails. [1]
- When threatened, it curls into an impenetrable armoured ball that even a tiger's jaws cannot crack open. [1]

### Uncertain or variable information
Wild longevity and exact population numbers remain uncertain because of extreme rarity and nocturnal secrecy.
