## At a glance

- Bird · Owl family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Length: 52–71 cm
- Wingspan: 1.25–1.5 m
- Weight: 1.6–3 kg
- Lifespan: 10+ yrs
- Home turf: Open treeless Arctic tundra, coastal dunes and winter prairies across the far north.
- On the menu: Lemmings (up to 1,600 a year per owl!), plus voles, Arctic hares and seabirds.
- Fun fact: Unlike most owls, Snowy Owls hunt during the day—essential during 24-hour Arctic summer sunlight!
- Fun fact: Thick feathers cover even their toes and claws like warm winter boots.
