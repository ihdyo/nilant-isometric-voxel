## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Pure white stout body with a bulbous echolocation melon on the forehead, distinct flexible neck crease, no dorsal fin, short broad paddle flippers, and graceful tail flukes. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 3.5–5.5 m; length 3.5–5.5 m, weight 1–1.5 t [1].

### Lifespan
35–50 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Salmon, Arctic cod, herring, shrimp, crabs, octopus, and benthic worms. [1].

### Habitat
Arctic and sub-Arctic coastal waters, estuaries, and shallow river mouths. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Nicknamed the 'canary of the sea' for its rich repertoire of chirps, whistles, clicks, and trills. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Unlike most whales, its neck vertebrae are unfused, allowing it to turn its head sideways and nod. [1].
