## At a glance

- Mammal · Monodontid whale family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 3.5–5.5 m
- Weight: 1–1.5 t
- Lifespan: 35–50 yrs
- Pregnancy: 14–15 months
- Home turf: Arctic and sub-Arctic coastal waters, estuaries, and shallow river mouths.
- On the menu: Salmon, Arctic cod, herring, shrimp, crabs, octopus, and benthic worms.
- Fun fact: Nicknamed the 'canary of the sea' for its rich repertoire of chirps, whistles, clicks, and trills.
- Fun fact: Unlike most whales, its neck vertebrae are unfused, allowing it to turn its head sideways and nod.
