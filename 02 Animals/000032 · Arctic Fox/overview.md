## At a glance

- Mammal · Dog family · IUCN Least Concern
- Body length: 46–68 cm
- Weight: 3–8 kg
- Lifespan: 3–6 yrs
- Pregnancy: 52 days
- Home turf: Treeless Arctic tundra, coastal pack ice and alpine fell across North America, Greenland, Iceland and Eurasia.
- On the menu: Lemmings, voles, seabirds, eggs, berries, carrion and leftovers from polar bear kills on sea ice.
- Fun fact: Its coat changes color with the seasons—pure snow-white in winter and charcoal-brown in summer.
- Fun fact: It can endure temperatures down to −70 °C without shivering thanks to the warmest fur of any mammal.
