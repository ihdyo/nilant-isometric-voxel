## At a glance

- Reptile · Elapid snake family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Length: 3–4 m
- Weight: 6–10 kg
- Lifespan: 20 yrs
- Clutch: 20–40 eggs
- Home turf: Dense tropical forests, bamboo thickets, and mangrove swamps across South and Southeast Asia.
- On the menu: Primarily other snakes—including pythons and rat snakes—plus occasional monitor lizards.
- Fun fact: The world's longest venomous snake, capable of rearing a third of its body upright to look an adult human in the eye.
- Fun fact: The only known snake species that builds an above-ground nest mound of dead leaves to guard its eggs.
