## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Long slender olive-black body with pale cream chevron bands, a narrow flared neck hood with a pale throat patch, large head scales, and a forked red tongue. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 3–4 m, up to 5.5 m (longest venomous snake); length 3–4 m, weight 6–10 kg [1].

### Lifespan
20 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Primarily other snakes—including pythons and rat snakes—plus occasional monitor lizards. [1].

### Habitat
Dense tropical forests, bamboo thickets, and mangrove swamps across South and Southeast Asia. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
The world's longest venomous snake, capable of rearing a third of its body upright to look an adult human in the eye. [1].

### Unique adaptations
The only known snake species that builds an above-ground nest mound of dead leaves to guard its eggs. [1].
