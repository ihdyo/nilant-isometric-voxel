## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Sleek jet-black body with a snow-white belly, white post-ocular eye patch, grey saddle patch behind a tall triangular dorsal fin, and broad paddle-shaped flippers. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 6–8 m in adult males, up to 9.8 m; length 6–8 m, weight 3.6–5.4 t [1].

### Lifespan
50–80 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Fish, squid, seals, sea lions, sharks, and whales depending on pod culture and ecotype. [1].

### Habitat
Every ocean on Earth, from Arctic and Antarctic pack ice to warm tropical seas. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
The largest member of the dolphin family, living in lifelong matrilineal pods with distinct vocal dialects. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Different pods pass down specialized hunting techniques across generations, such as wave-washing seals off ice floes. [1].
