## At a glance

- Mammal · Oceanic dolphin family · IUCN Data Deficient
- Length: 6–8 m
- Weight: 3.6–5.4 t
- Lifespan: 50–80 yrs
- Pregnancy: 15–18 months
- Home turf: Every ocean on Earth, from Arctic and Antarctic pack ice to warm tropical seas.
- On the menu: Fish, squid, seals, sea lions, sharks, and whales depending on pod culture and ecotype.
- Fun fact: The largest member of the dolphin family, living in lifelong matrilineal pods with distinct vocal dialects.
