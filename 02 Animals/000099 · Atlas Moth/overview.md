## At a glance

- Insect · Giant silk moth family · IUCN Not Evaluated
- Wingspan: 24–27 cm
- Weight: 4–8 g
- Lifespan: 1–2 weeks
- Eggs: 100–200
- Home turf: Tropical and subtropical forests across Southeast Asia, southern China, India, and the Malay Archipelago.
- On the menu: Citrus, cinnamon, and rambutan leaves as caterpillars; adults have no functional mouthparts and do not eat.
- Fun fact: Both forewing tips are shaped and patterned like snake heads to startle hungry birds and lizards.
- Fun fact: Adult moths live entirely on fat reserves stored during their caterpillar stage, surviving only one to two weeks.
