## At a glance

- Mammal · Tapir family · IUCN Endangered
- Body length: 1.8–2.5 m
- Weight: 250–320 kg
- Lifespan: 25–30 yrs
- Pregnancy: 390–400 days
- Home turf: Tropical lowland and hill rainforests across Sumatra, Peninsular Malaysia, southern Thailand and southern Myanmar.
- On the menu: Tender shoots, leaves, twigs, aquatic plants and fallen forest fruits gathered with its flexible trunk-like snout.
- Fun fact: Its bold black-and-white saddle looks striking in daylight, yet breaks up its outline like boulders in moonlit jungle shadows.
- Fun fact: The largest of the four tapir species and the only one native to Asia, closely related to horses and rhinoceroses.
