## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Golf-ball-sized golden-ochre body and eight short arms covered in dark-edged iridescent electric-blue rings. [1].

### Size and weight
Mantle length ~5 cm, total length with arms 10–12 cm; length 10–12 cm, weight 10–30 g [1].

### Lifespan
1–2 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Small crabs, hermit crabs, and shrimp hunted at night on reef flats. [1].

### Habitat
Shallow coral reefs, tide pools, and rubble flats across northern Australia, Indonesia, and the Coral Triangle. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Flashes around 50 to 60 iridescent blue rings in a fraction of a second as a warning display when threatened. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Carries symbiotic bacteria that produce tetrodotoxin, a potent neurotoxin over 1,000 times stronger than cyanide. [1].
