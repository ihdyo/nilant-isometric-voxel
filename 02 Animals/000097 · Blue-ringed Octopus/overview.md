## At a glance

- Mollusc · Octopus family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 10–12 cm
- Weight: 10–30 g
- Lifespan: 1–2 yrs
- Eggs: 50–100
- Home turf: Shallow coral reefs, tide pools, and rubble flats across northern Australia, Indonesia, and the Coral Triangle.
- On the menu: Small crabs, hermit crabs, and shrimp hunted at night on reef flats.
- Fun fact: Flashes around 50 to 60 iridescent blue rings in a fraction of a second as a warning display when threatened.
- Fun fact: Carries symbiotic bacteria that produce tetrodotoxin, a potent neurotoxin over 1,000 times stronger than cyanide.
