## At a glance

- Bird · Hawk & eagle family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 70–102 cm
- Wingspan: 1.8–2.3 m
- Weight: 3–6.3 kg
- Lifespan: 20–30 yrs
- Home turf: Coasts, rivers, large lakes and reservoirs bordered by tall old-growth trees across North America.
- On the menu: Fish (especially salmon, trout and catfish) snatched from the water surface, plus waterfowl and carrion.
- Fun fact: They build the largest tree nests of any bird—up to 4 metres deep and weighing over a metric tonne!
- Fun fact: Juveniles have mottled brown heads and don't develop their iconic white head and tail until around age five.
