## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Robust cylindrical body in bluish-grey skin speckled with bright brick-orange and pale blue spots, large vertical-pupil golden eyes without eyelids, and wide expanded toe pads. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 25–35 cm from snout to tail tip; length 25–35 cm, weight 150–300 g [1].

### Lifespan
10–15 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Moths, beetles, cockroaches, spiders, and small vertebrates hunted nocturnally. [1].

### Habitat
Rainforest trees, rocky cliffs, and rural buildings across Southeast Asia, India, and Indonesia. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Named after its loud, echoing 'to-kay' mating call that can be heard more than 100 metres away. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Millions of microscopic hair-like setae on its toe pads let it sprint upside down across smooth glass. [1].
