- Home turf: Rainforest trees, rocky cliffs, and rural buildings across Southeast Asia, India, and Indonesia.
- On the menu: Moths, beetles, cockroaches, spiders, and small vertebrates hunted nocturnally.
- Fun fact: Named after its loud, echoing 'to-kay' mating call that can be heard more than 100 metres away.
