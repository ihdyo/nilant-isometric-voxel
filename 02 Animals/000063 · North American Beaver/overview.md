## At a glance

- Mammal · Beaver family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 0.9–1.2 m
- Weight: 11–32 kg
- Lifespan: 10–15 yrs
- Dive time: Up to 15 mins
- Home turf: Freshwater streams, rivers, ponds, marshes and wooded lakes across North America.
- On the menu: Inner bark and twigs of aspen, willow, birch and cottonwood, plus water lilies and pond plants.
- Fun fact: Nature's master ecosystem engineer—it builds dams of logs and mud that create thriving wetland habitats for hundreds of species.
- Fun fact: Its front teeth are bright orange because their enamel is reinforced with iron, making them self-sharpening.
