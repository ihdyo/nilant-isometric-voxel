## At a glance

- Bird · Auk family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Length: 26–30 cm
- Wingspan: 47–63 cm
- Weight: 320–480 g
- Lifespan: 20+ yrs
- Home turf: Cold North Atlantic oceans in winter, nesting on grassy sea cliffs and islands in spring and summer.
- On the menu: Small marine fish—especially sand eels, herring and capelin—caught by 'flying' underwater.
- Fun fact: Backward-pointing spines on its tongue and palate let it hold up to 60 small fish crosswise in its beak at once!
- Fun fact: Its famous rainbow bill is only worn in summer—in winter it sheds the outer plates and turns smaller and dull grey.
