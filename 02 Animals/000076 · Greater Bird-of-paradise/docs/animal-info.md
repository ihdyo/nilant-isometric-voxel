## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Rich chestnut-brown body, bright yellow crown and nape, iridescent emerald-green throat, pale blue-grey bill, and dramatic golden-orange flank plumes with two long black tail wires in adult males. [1].

### Size and weight
Body length 43–44 cm (excluding long ornamental tail wires up to ~75 cm); length 43–44 cm, weight 170–300 g [1].

### Lifespan
15–30 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Forest fruits, berries, and canopy arthropods such as insects and spiders. [1].

### Habitat
Lowland and hill rainforests of southern New Guinea and the Aru Islands, from sea level up to 1,000 m. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Males gather in canopy leks and flare cascading golden-yellow flank plumes to impress watching females. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Early European traders thought it had no feet because trade skins lacked legs—hence apoda, meaning 'footless'. [1].
