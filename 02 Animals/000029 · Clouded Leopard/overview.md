## At a glance

- Mammal · Cat family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Body length: 0.69–1.08 m
- Weight: 11–23 kg
- Lifespan: 11–17 yrs
- Pregnancy: 85–93 days
- Home turf: Dense tropical evergreen and deciduous forests from the Himalayan foothills across mainland Southeast Asia.
- On the menu: Monkeys, gibbons, squirrels, slow lorises, deer, wild pigs and forest birds hunted in trees and on the ground.
- Fun fact: Rotating hind ankles let it climb down tree trunks headfirst and hang upside down beneath branches.
- Fun fact: It has the longest upper canine teeth relative to skull size of any living wild cat—up to 4 cm long.
