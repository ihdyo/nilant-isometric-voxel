## At a glance

- Mammal · Wombat family · IUCN Least Concern
- Length: 0.85–1.15 m
- Weight: 22–39 kg
- Lifespan: 15–20 yrs
- Top speed: 40 km/h
- Home turf: Eucalyptus forests, coastal heathlands and cooler mountain woodlands of southeastern Australia and Tasmania.
- On the menu: Tough native tussock grasses, sedges, roots and bark grazed at night.
- Fun fact: The only known animal in the world that produces cube-shaped droppings—so they don't roll away when marking territory!
- Fun fact: Its rear end is reinforced with thick cartilage plates; when threatened, it blocks its burrow with its armoured rump.
