## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Glossy black armoured thorax and legs, olive-lime elytra (wing covers) speckled with black spots, and two opposing forward-curving pincer horns. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 5–17 cm in adult males including the long thoracic horn; length 5–17 cm, weight 30–100 g [1].

### Lifespan
1–2 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Decaying wood as larvae; fermenting fallen tropical fruit and tree sap as adults. [1].

### Habitat
Humid montane and lowland rainforests from southern Mexico through Central America to Bolivia and Brazil. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Males sport a giant thoracic horn longer than their body, which they use like pincers to flip rivals off tree branches. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Its wing covers change color from olive-yellow in dry air to black in high humidity due to light-refracting micro-structures. [1].
