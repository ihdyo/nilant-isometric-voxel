## At a glance

- Insect · Scarab beetle family · IUCN Not Evaluated
- Length: 5–17 cm
- Weight: 30–100 g
- Lifespan: 1–2 yrs
- Eggs: 50–100
- Home turf: Humid montane and lowland rainforests from southern Mexico through Central America to Bolivia and Brazil.
- On the menu: Decaying wood as larvae; fermenting fallen tropical fruit and tree sap as adults.
- Fun fact: Males sport a giant thoracic horn longer than their body, which they use like pincers to flip rivals off tree branches.
- Fun fact: Its wing covers change color from olive-yellow in dry air to black in high humidity due to light-refracting micro-structures.
