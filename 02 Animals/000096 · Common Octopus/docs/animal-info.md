## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Bulbous sac-like mantle head, prominent horizontal-pupil eyes, side siphon funnel, and eight flexible arms lined with double rows of pale suction cups. [1].

### Size and weight
Total arm span 0.6–1.0 m, up to 1.3 m; span 0.6–1 m, weight 1–3 kg [1].

### Lifespan
1–2 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Crabs, lobsters, clams, scallops, snails, and small benthic fish. [1].

### Habitat
Coastal rocky reefs, seagrass beds, and sandy seafloors across temperate and tropical oceans worldwide. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
Has three hearts, blue copper-based blood, and roughly two-thirds of its neurons distributed inside its eight arms. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Can match both the color and bumpy texture of surrounding rocks in less than a second using chromatophores and papillae. [1].
