## Biological characteristics

### Physical characteristics
Enormous streamlined blue-grey body mottled with lighter patches, broad flat U-shaped rostrum, pleated ventral throat grooves, long tapered flippers, tiny dorsal fin set far back, and wide tail flukes. [1].

### Size and weight
Total length 24–30 m (largest animal ever known to have lived on earth); length 24–30 m, weight 100–150 t [1].

### Lifespan
80–90 yrs [1].

### Diet and feeding behaviour
Almost exclusively krill—filtering up to 4 tonnes of tiny crustaceans per day through baleen plates. [1].

### Habitat
Open oceans worldwide from polar feeding grounds to tropical breeding waters. [1].

### Habits and behaviour
The largest animal ever known to exist on Earth, with a heart roughly the size of a small car. [1].

### Unique adaptations
Its low-frequency songs reach up to 188 decibels and can travel hundreds of kilometres underwater. [1].
