## At a glance

- Mammal · Baleen whale family · IUCN Endangered
- Length: 24–30 m
- Weight: 100–150 t
- Lifespan: 80–90 yrs
- Pregnancy: 10–12 months
- Home turf: Open oceans worldwide from polar feeding grounds to tropical breeding waters.
- On the menu: Almost exclusively krill—filtering up to 4 tonnes of tiny crustaceans per day through baleen plates.
- Fun fact: The largest animal ever known to exist on Earth, with a heart roughly the size of a small car.
- Fun fact: Its low-frequency songs reach up to 188 decibels and can travel hundreds of kilometres underwater.
