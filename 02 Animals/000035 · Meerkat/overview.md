## At a glance

- Mammal · Mongoose family · IUCN Least Concern
- Body length: 25–35 cm
- Weight: 0.6–0.97 kg
- Lifespan: 12–14 yrs
- Pregnancy: 70 days
- Home turf: Arid open savannas, scrublands and Kalahari desert plains across southern Africa.
- On the menu: Beetles, scorpions, spiders, centipedes, lizards, small snakes, eggs and desert tubers.
- Fun fact: While the mob digs for insects, one sentry stands upright on its tail and calls distinct alarms for eagles versus jackals.
- Fun fact: Dark rings around its eyes act like built-in sunglasses against the bright Kalahari sun.
