## At a glance

- Mammal · Civet family · IUCN Vulnerable
- Body length: 61–96 cm
- Weight: 9–20 kg
- Lifespan: 15–20 yrs
- Pregnancy: 90–92 days
- Home turf: Dense primary and secondary tropical rainforests across mainland Southeast Asia, Sumatra, Java, Borneo and Palawan.
- On the menu: Strangler figs, wild fruits, shoots, leaves, insects, birds, eggs and small forest mammals.
- Fun fact: Scent glands under its tail release a compound (2-AP) that smells remarkably like warm buttered popcorn.
- Fun fact: It is one of only two carnivores—alongside the kinkajou—with a truly prehensile tail strong enough to support its full weight.
